What it is

One directory, not a dozen group chats.

4Ummah is a directory of products built by Muslims, and roles at companies we have reviewed. Finding either currently depends on knowing the right person, in the right chat, at the right moment. That is a poor way to discover anything.

The products do not have to be religious. A Qur'an app belongs here, and so does a design tool. The bar is that it was built by Muslims and that we are comfortable listing it.

What is in it

Two things first. More once those are right.

  • Products

    Apps, tools and companies built by Muslims. Browsable, searchable, and organised by what they actually do.

    First

  • Jobs

    Roles at companies we have reviewed, so the search does not start with wondering whether a place fits your values.

    First

  • News

    Later

  • Events

    Later

  • Community

We would rather do two things properly than five things badly.

Who it is for

Three people, one directory.

  • Anyone looking

    You want a Qur'an app that is actually good, or a job somewhere you will not have to explain yourself. Browse it. No account needed.

  • People who build

    You made something and nobody can find it. List it, keep it current, and see how many people we send your way.

  • Companies hiring

    You want people who share your values. Post a role and reach them directly.
